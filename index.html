How to study before the exam
  • First pass: read a set once for meaning, then close the answer and retrieve each formula from memory. Say what each variable means.
  • Practice: do the numerical example without looking. Mark “I knew it” only if you can recall the formula and explain when to use it.
  • Repeat: missed cards return after the other unmastered cards in your set; mastered cards stay at the back. Finish all sets, then restart the chapter round to revisit the full stack.
  • Space sessions: revisit missed formulas later the same day, the next day, and again before the exam. Mix topics once the basics feel familiar; take short breaks.
  • 7–11 November: cover the sets in manageable blocks, revisit weaker chapters daily, and use 11 November for mixed recall and rest. This page does not schedule reminders or measure retention over time.
