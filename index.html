Study plan & focus tips
  • One small start: pick one set, put distractions away and try the optional 10-minute timer. Pause whenever you need; experiment with a short movement or water break between blocks.
  • Recall and apply: write the formula, explain its variables and when to use it, then solve the example before revealing its solution. Mark “I knew it” only when you can do all three.
  • Repeat: missed cards return after other unmastered cards; known cards stay at the back. Finish sets and restart a chapter round to revisit its full stack.
  • 7–11 November: use the suggested daily pace as a flexible guide, not a deadline for each card. Revisit weaker cards later the same day and on later days; mix topics and leave time for exam-style questions and rest. The app does not schedule or track spaced reviews.
  • Focus support: short blocks and visible next steps may help reduce friction, including for some people with ADHD, but individual preferences differ. This is not medical advice or an ADHD treatment.

Research on learning in general: Karpicke & Roediger (2008), retrieval practice; Cepeda et al. (2006), spaced practice. These studies do not establish an ADHD-specific timer duration.
