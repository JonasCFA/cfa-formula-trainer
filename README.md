# CFA Level I Formula Trainer

An independent, English-language study companion with 153 formula flashcards, numerical examples, related formulas and chapter progress across nine topics.

Open `index.html` in a browser to study locally; keep `formulas.js`, `challenges.js`, and `challenges.html` alongside it. No installation or internet connection is needed. Progress is stored in the browser's local storage and is specific to the browser and page origin. Moving between a local file and a hosted page may result in separate progress.

The dashboard shows self-assessed chapter-round progress, a flexible daily goal and practice points (10 per distinct card marked known per day). Restarting a chapter resets its round progress but does not erase practice points. The optional 10-minute focus timer runs only while the page is open. Worked examples separate the prompt and solution so learners can attempt the calculation first. Suggested pacing targets 11 November 2026 and is not a spaced-repetition scheduler.

The separate Formula Chain Challenges page offers 23 representative multi-step questions across all nine chapters, with optional hints, individually revealed solution steps and links to relevant formula cards. Each step explains why and how the formula is used; it is not one separate challenge per formula card. Challenge practice does not affect card progress or points; selecting a linked card opens its chapter in read mode.

This is a selected formula collection, not a complete CFA curriculum or a reproduction of the linked cheat sheets. Check formula conventions against your curriculum.
